How Much Is That Leak Costing You? (Moulton Niguel Water District Rates)

Most online drip calculators use a generic national water rate. This one uses Moulton Niguel Water District rates and accounts for MNWD's water budget system, where a leak that pushes monthly use over your indoor budget is billed at higher Tier 2 and above rates. To stop a leak from running up your bill further, call (949) 325-3122 for same-day repair.

MNWD wastewater is based on household size, not usage, so a leak does not raise the wastewater portion of the bill.
