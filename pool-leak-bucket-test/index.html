Pool Leak or Just Evaporation? Aliso Viejo Pool Bucket Test Calculator

Aliso Viejo pools lose water to evaporation year-round under the Southern California sun. The bucket test is the most reliable way to separate a real leak from normal evaporation before calling a technician. If the test confirms a leak, call (949) 325-3122 for professional pool leak detection.

With MNWD water budget-based pricing, a leaking pool that pushes monthly water use over your outdoor budget can trigger higher-tier pricing, making a repair worth pursuing even at the district's low base rates.

How to Run the Bucket Test

  1. 1Fill a 5-gallon bucket with pool water to about 1 inch from the top.
  2. 2Place the bucket on the first or second pool step so the water level inside matches the pool water level outside.
  3. 3Mark the inside bucket level and the outside pool level with tape or a marker.
  4. 4Run the pool pump on its normal schedule. Disable auto-fill. Wait 24 hours.
  5. 5Measure how much the level dropped inside the bucket and outside. Enter both below.

Turning off auto-fill is critical. If auto-fill is active, it compensates for any leak and the test will show no difference even if the pool is leaking significantly.
