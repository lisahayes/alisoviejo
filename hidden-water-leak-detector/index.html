Find a Hidden Water Leak in Your Aliso Viejo Home (Water Meter Test)

The water meter is the most reliable way to confirm a hidden leak before calling a plumber. Moulton Niguel Water District uses a personalized budget-based rate system, so a leak that pushes monthly use over your indoor budget enters higher pricing tiers. Even at MNWD rates, which are among the lowest in South Orange County, a leak adds up quickly once it exceeds the indoor water budget. If your meter confirms an active leak, call (949) 325-3122 for same-day acoustic detection.

MNWD wastewater is charged on household size, not water volume. A supply leak does not raise the wastewater portion of your bill.

How to Run the Test

  1. 1Turn off every water fixture and appliance in the home, including the irrigation system.
  2. 2Locate your MNWD water meter at the curb and read the current number in HCF or gallons.
  3. 3Wait at least 2 hours without using any water.
  4. 4Read the meter again and enter both numbers below.

About MNWD Budget-Based Rates (2026)

Moulton Niguel Water District uses a 5-tier budget system. Every household gets a personalized indoor and outdoor water budget based on household size and landscaped area. Usage within the indoor budget is Tier 1 ($2.76/HCF). Exceeding the total budget moves into Tier 2 ($3.34), Tier 3 ($4.75), Tier 4 ($7.26), or Tier 5 ($11.31). A $0.14/HCF wholesale pass-through applies to all tiers since January 2025.

Wastewater is a flat monthly service charge ($28.88) plus a per-person fee ($5.89/person) based on household size. A leak does not raise your wastewater bill. If a confirmed leak pushed your bill into Tier 3 or 4, you may qualify for recalculation at the Tier 2 rate via the MNWD Bill Adjustment Program.
